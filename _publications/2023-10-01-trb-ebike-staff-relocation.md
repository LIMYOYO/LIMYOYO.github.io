We develop a staff-based operational strategy for e-bike sharing systems, integrating e-bike relocation and battery swapping through unified optimization. We model e-bike and battery station dynamics with a Markov chain, tracking fleet size and battery levels. We propose an adaptive one-step Markovian strategy and a globally optimal rolling-horizon strategy. Empirical validation on real-world data demonstrates over 20% profit improvement against industrial baselines.
