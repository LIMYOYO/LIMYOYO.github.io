We build a Networked Cournot model to study competition under crowd-sourced labor in rental networks. We analyze firm profits, labor surplus, and consumer surplus under firm sourcing, crowd sourcing, and dual sourcing, using comparative statics to identify when crowd-sourced labor benefits different stakeholders.

**Status:** Working paper — Reject & Resubmit (Invited) at *Manufacturing & Service Operations Management (MSOM)*
