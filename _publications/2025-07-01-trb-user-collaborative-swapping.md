We study user-collaborative battery swapping strategies for shared electric micromobility systems, with the goal of supporting sustainable urban mobility. This paper develops mechanisms that engage users in the rebalancing process for electric shared bike fleets.

**Status:** Submitted to *Transportation Research Part B: Methodological* (Manuscript No. TRB-D-25-01174)
